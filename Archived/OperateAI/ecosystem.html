Blockchain Partnership

Built on Accumulate Network, the identity and data based blockchain

Accumulate Network Partnership

  • AccumulateNetwork.io is an identity and data based blockchain purpose-built for decentralized identity, key management, and on-chain data organization.
  • Premier company: DefiDevs.io drives the growth, development, and adoption of the Accumulate blockchain ecosystem.
  • BizFirst has acquired approximately 10% of available Accumulate coins, establishing significant governance influence over the protocol's direction.
  • BizFirst controls more than 10% stake in DefiDevs, ensuring strategic alignment between the AI ecosystem and the underlying blockchain infrastructure.
  • OperateAI also integrates with Ethereum, Bitcoin, and other major blockchains through the Accumulate bridge, enabling cross-chain interoperability for AI agents.
The deep integration with Accumulate Network provides OperateAI with human-readable addressing, hierarchical identity management, and a unique key-book architecture that allows AI agents to operate with real-world governance models.
