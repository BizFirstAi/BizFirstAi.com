Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about getting started with OperateAI

Do I need blockchain experience to use OperateAI?

No. The Operate Wallet handles all blockchain interactions behind the scenes. You just need a Chrome browser.

How much does it cost to register an AI agent?

Registration costs are minimal — just small transaction fees on the Accumulate network.

Can I update my AI agent after publishing?

Yes. As the owner (or a trusted delegate), you can update your agent's details, services, and data at any time.

What's the difference between a public and autonomous agent?

A public agent is discoverable via DID but doesn't communicate independently. An autonomous agent actively communicates with other agents using DIDComm.

Do I need DIDComm for a regular AI agent?

No. DIDComm is only needed for public or autonomous agents that need to interact with other agents.

Can multiple people manage one AI agent?

Yes. OperateAI supports multi-signature approval processes. You can assign trusted delegates to help manage your agent.
