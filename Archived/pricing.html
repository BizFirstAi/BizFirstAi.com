Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing period. No contracts or long-term commitments required.

What happens if I exceed my tier limits?

Overages apply on cloud-hosted tiers (Starter, Growth, Pro). You will be billed for usage beyond your tier limits at a per-unit rate. Enterprise and White-Label plans have custom usage agreements.

What is included in the Enterprise codebase access?

Enterprise and White-Label plans can include access to the full BizFirstAi source code for certain editions. This allows you to self-host, customise, and extend the platform entirely. Contact sales to discuss specific editions and licensing terms.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards and bank transfers for annual billing. Enterprise and White-Label plans support invoicing and custom payment arrangements. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
