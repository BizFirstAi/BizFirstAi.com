The challenge

Building a marketplace from
scratch takes years.

SaaS companies that want Salesforce AppExchange-style ecosystems discover that the engineering challenge is enormous — and the hidden costs are even bigger.

Time & Cost

12 to 24 months of senior engineering time just to reach a working v1. That's before onboarding publishers, moderating listings, or managing licences at scale. Most teams underestimate this by 3x.

Quality Risk

Security, multi-tenancy, licensing enforcement, publisher verification — every one of these is a discipline on its own. Homegrown solutions consistently accumulate technical debt and introduce attack surface.

Ecosystem Lock-In

Proprietary marketplace SaaS locks you into their runtime, pricing, and roadmap. If they change terms or shut down, your publishers and customers have nowhere to go. You need code you own.

Core modules

Six modules. Every capability.

Each module ships as a clean, independently-testable domain — so you can extend or replace individual capabilities without disrupting the rest of the platform.

Catalogue

SemVer package management with dependency resolution, validation rules, bulk import/export, and 20+ API endpoints for full lifecycle control.

Discovery

TF-IDF search ranking engine combining text relevance (40%), download velocity (30%), star rating (20%), and recency (10%) for accurate, fair results.

Distribution

Four licence types — Trial, Subscription, Perpetual, and Metered — with geo-restrictions, seat limits, auto-renewal, and a full audit trail.

Community

Threaded discussions, nested comments, @mentions, follow system, moderation tools, and a badge system — driving engagement inside your marketplace.

Publisher

Structured verification pipeline (Unverified → Pending → Verified → Certified), 4-dimensional reputation scoring with vesting cliffs to prevent rating gaming.

Reviews

Community-driven ratings and written reviews with sentiment analysis, content moderation, and publisher response capabilities to maintain quality standards.
