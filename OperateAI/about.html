About OperateAI

The decentralized AI Registry platform by BizFirst AI — powering sovereign AI agent identity through strategic blockchain partnerships and a growing ecosystem of owned properties.

Our Mission

OperateAI exists to give individuals and organizations full sovereignty over their AI agent identities. We believe AI agents should be registered, certified, and discoverable on-chain — controlled only by their owners, not by any government or corporation. Through decentralized identity, multi-signature governance, and on-chain certification, OperateAI is building the trust layer for the autonomous AI economy.

Why These Stakes Matter

  • BizFirst selected Accumulate Network as its blockchain foundation for its identity-based addressing and hierarchical key management
  • The ~10% coin acquisition gives BizFirst governance influence to prioritize features needed by OperateAI
  • The DefiDevs stake ensures that core protocol development aligns with OperateAI's roadmap
