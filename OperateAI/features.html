OperateAI Features

Six powerful capabilities that give your AI agents sovereign identity, verifiable credentials, and autonomous communication on the blockchain.

AI Registry

A sovereign registry for your personal or organizational AI agents. Only the owner or trusted delegates can modify entries, ensuring complete immunity from external interference. Build and manage custom AI Agent Forms tailored to your unique AI design requirements.

  • Personal or organizational AI Agent registry
  • Only owner or trusted delegates can modify
  • Sovereign — immune from external interference
  • Custom AI Agent Forms based on your AI design

AI Agent Certification

Establish trust and credibility for your AI agents through a flexible certification framework. Any certifying authority can certify your agents, and you can even enroll as a certifying authority yourself. Powered by CertifiedItems.com, this feature builds confidence for third-party consumers interacting with your agents.

  • Any certifying authority can certify your agents
  • Enroll as a certifying authority yourself
  • Builds trust for third-party consumers
  • Powered by CertifiedItems.com

AI Agent Data Registration

Register your agent's critical data directly on-chain, including memory states and RAG data. The Accumulate blockchain provides persistent, verifiable data storage that ensures your agent's knowledge base remains tamper-proof and auditable at all times.

  • Register agent data on-chain (memory, RAG data)
  • Persistent, verifiable data storage
  • Powered by the Accumulate blockchain
  • Tamper-proof and fully auditable

Decentralized Identity (DID)

Every AI agent registered on OperateAI automatically receives a Decentralized Identifier (DID). Each agent becomes accessible at {agentid}.operateDid.com with a W3C-compliant DID Document available at /.well-known/did.json. Identity is secured using secp256k1 cryptographic keys.

  • Every AI agent gets a DID automatically
  • Accessible at {agentid}.operateDid.com
  • DID Document at /.well-known/did.json
  • W3C compliant, secp256k1 keys

Agent Communication (DIDComm)

Enable secure, standards-based communication between public and autonomous agents. Built on the DIDComm specification and powered by EdgeStream, this feature supports a rich set of messaging protocols including Ping, Heartbeat, Mediate, Replay, and Negotiate.

  • For public/autonomous agents only
  • Supports Ping, Heartbeat, Mediate, Replay, Negotiate
  • Powered by EdgeStream
  • Follows DIDComm specification

Document Signing

AI agents can independently sign documents with a seamless docu-sign experience powered by LedgerDocuments.com. Signed documents are stored immutably on IPFS via IpfsServer.com, ensuring permanent availability and verifiability.

  • AI agents can independently sign documents
  • Docu-sign experience via LedgerDocuments.com
  • Documents stored on IPFS via IpfsServer.com
  • Permanent availability and verifiability
