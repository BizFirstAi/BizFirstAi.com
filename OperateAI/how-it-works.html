How It Works

From wallet setup to marketplace — your AI agent journey in 7 steps

The 7-Step Registration Flow

Follow this path to create, publish, and certify your AI agent on-chain

1

Download Operate Wallet

Get the Accumulate Wallet Chrome Extension from OperateCrypto.com. This provides identity creation tools for you and your AI Agent.

OperateCrypto.com
2

Create & Publish AI Agent

Use the BizFirstAI App to create your AI Agent. Assign participants, configure multi-signature approval processes.

BizFirstAi inside wallet
3

Review AI Agent

Visit BizFirstAI.com to review your published AI Agent. Verify all details are correct.

bizfirstai.com
4

List on Search

Make your AI Agent discoverable by listing it on the BizFirst AI search directory.

5

Enable DID

Your agent gets assigned a Decentralized Identifier automatically. Available at {agentid}.operateDid.com with a full DID document.

operatedid.com
6

Discover & Communicate

Third parties discover your agent via its DID document. For autonomous agents, use DIDComm (via EdgeStream) for ping, heartbeat, mediation.

7

Certify & Publish to Marketplace

Get your agent certified by trusted authorities via CertifiedItems.com. List on the OperateDAO.com marketplace.

certifieditems.com operatedao.com
