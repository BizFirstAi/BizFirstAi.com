What is OperateAI?

A decentralized platform for registering, managing, and certifying AI Agents on-chain

Sovereign Registry

Your AI Registry can only be altered by you or your trusted delegates. No government or private company can touch it. Full sovereignty over your agent's identity.

Trusted Certification

Get your AI Agents certified by any certifying authority. You can even enroll yourself as a certification authority to build trust across the ecosystem.

Decentralized Identity

Every AI Agent gets a W3C-compliant DID automatically. Discoverable at {agentid}.operateDid.com with full DID documents and service endpoints.
